# Rabbit Hole Map — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-08_

Rabbit Hole Map draws your browsing trail as a visual map, **entirely on your
own computer**.

## What the extension stores

To draw the map, the extension records — **locally, in your browser's
extension storage only**:

- the URL, title, and visit count of pages you visit while it's installed
- which page led to which (the links you clicked, including into new tabs)
- timestamps, used to group browsing into sessions

## What leaves your device

**Nothing.** The extension has no servers, makes no network requests, and
contains no analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind. Your browsing data
never leaves your browser. No account exists and none is needed.

## What is never recorded

- Anything you do in **Incognito** windows
- Pages on domains you add to the **blocklist** in the extension's Options
- Non-web pages (browser settings pages, local files, other extensions)

## Your controls

- **Blocklist** any domain in Options — it and its subdomains are never recorded.
- **Clear all data** in Options deletes every recorded session instantly.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version ships with the extension
update that changes it, with the date above revised.
